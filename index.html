SIT Tumkur - Academic Rules & Regulations (2025-26)

Autonomous Institution

Attendance Regulations

  • Minimum Attendance: A student must maintain a minimum of 85% attendance in each course in a semester to be eligible to appear for the Semester End Examination (SEE).
  • Condonation Criteria: The Principal may condone attendance shortage up to 10% (down to 75% minimum) on recommendation of the Head of the Department (HoD) for genuine reasons (medical emergencies or participation in national/international sports/co-curricular activities).
  • Supporting Documents: To claim condonation, official medical certificates or representation letters must be submitted within 3 working days of returning to classes.
  • Detention Consequences (NE): Students having less than 75% attendance, or between 75% and 84% without approved condonation, are declared "Not Eligible" (NE). They are detained in that subject and must repeat it.

Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE)

  • Total Weightage: CIE constitutes 50% (50 marks) of the total assessment for a course.
  • Theory Courses Components: Typically assessed through:
    - Two Written IA Tests (max 20 marks each).
    - Assignments / Quizzes / Mini-projects (max 10 marks total).
  • CIE Passing Threshold: A student must obtain a minimum of 40% in CIE (i.e., 20 out of 50 marks) to be eligible to appear for the Semester End Examination (SEE) in that course.
  • Low CIE Status: Students scoring less than 20/50 in CIE are declared ineligible (NE) to write the exam and are detained in the course.

Semester End Examination (SEE)

  • Total Weightage: SEE constitutes 50% (50 marks scaled down from 100) of the total assessment.
  • SEE Passing Threshold: A student must obtain a minimum of 40% in SEE (i.e., 40 out of 100 marks, which scales to 20 out of 50 marks) to pass the course.
  • Cumulative Passing Criteria: To pass a course, the overall combined score of CIE and SEE must be at least 40% (40 out of 100 marks).
